What RotWatch is

RotWatch checks web pages. In this version, you can run a one-off check of a single page. Scheduled monitoring, email alerts, and weekly reports are planned and are not part of the service yet. The home page will say when each one is live.

Accounts and records

There are no accounts in this version. RotWatch keeps no history of the checks you run.

Fair use

Check pages you own or have permission to check. Do not use the checker to flood other sites with requests. RotWatch may limit how often one address can run checks.

Results

A check result is a reading taken at one moment. A page can go down after a check passes, and a link can return a different status on the next request. RotWatch does not promise that a check result will stay true.
