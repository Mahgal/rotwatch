Privacy

Last updated: November 18, 2026.

What you submit

When you run a check, RotWatch fetches the address you submit. The address is used for that check only. RotWatch does not store it, and it keeps no history of checks.

What the host records

RotWatch runs on Cloudflare. Cloudflare keeps standard server logs, which can include the visitor address, the time of the request, and the requested address. Those logs are covered by Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

RotWatch sets no cookies and installs no tracking scripts. The only script on this site is the one that runs the check form.

Email addresses

RotWatch collects no email addresses in this version. Email alerts are planned. When they open, this page will say what address is collected and how it is used.

Changes

This page may change. The date at the top shows the last update.

Contact

RotWatch collects no contact details in this version. A contact address will be added here when accounts open.
